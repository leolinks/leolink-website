Industries

Behavioral health is home base. The door is open.

We go deepest where training hours, credentials, exclusion screening, and audits are part of ordinary operations. The same custom apps, automation, and support fit other small businesses.

Primary focus

Behavioral and mental health

Programs on this list each carry their own documentation. We configure training, HR, compliance, screening, and audits around those rules, including COMAR, CARF, and The Joint Commission.

  • Outpatient mental health centers
  • Psychiatric rehabilitation programs (PRP)
  • Health homes and care coordination
  • Substance use disorder programs
  • Group homes and residential programs
  • Crisis, respite, and community-based services
  • Day programs and partial hospitalization

What the work usually involves

Provable training hours, licenses that expire on a schedule, monthly exclusion screening, and chart audits against program rules. Learning Hub, Compliance Hub, Staff Exclusion Audit, and Chart Audit are built for that pattern.
