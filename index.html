The challenge

Care, administration, and HR on the same desk.

Leaders are asked to deliver services and to prove the work around them. Training hours, credentials, exclusion screening, and audits all have to be ready when someone asks.

Training hours

Annual hours and role-based courses that can be shown, including certificates earned outside the course list.

Credentials

Licenses and certificates with expirations visible before a lapse becomes a finding.

Exclusion screening

Monthly checks of active staff, with a person confirming any possible match.

Audits

Chart and record review against the rules of the program, early enough to act.

How we help

Three ways the load comes off the team.

Technology does the repetitive work. Consulting keeps the process honest. A person stays in control of the decisions that matter.

Custom web apps

Secure browser-based tools for training, HR, compliance, and operations, configured to your workflows and your branding.

Automation & integration

Bots for repetitive checks, audits, and data moves. A person is always in control of the final decision.

Consulting & support

Process mapping, technology planning, and ongoing support — including virtual assistance, business consulting, grant administration, and licensing and compliance.
