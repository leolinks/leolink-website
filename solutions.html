Learning Hub

Live demo

A learning management system for staff training. It tracks the 40-hour annual requirement, assigns onboarding, annual, and continuing education courses by role, and gives administrators a compliance view shaped for CARF, COMAR, and The Joint Commission.

  • 40-hour annual training tracked in one place
  • Onboarding, annual, and continuing education courses assigned by role
  • Quizzes to confirm the material was completed
  • PDF certificates, plus uploads for certificates earned outside the system
  • An admin compliance dashboard for CARF, COMAR, and TJC reviews

In use at a Maryland behavioral health provider. The live demo uses a fictional sample agency.

HR Management System

Employee records and documents, onboarding checklists, and tracking for credentials and license expirations. HR system integration connects the record to BambooHR and to training and compliance, so a hire, a role change, or an upcoming expiration shows up where people already work.

  • Employee records and the documents that belong with them
  • Onboarding checklists that follow the role
  • Credential and license expiry tracking
  • HR system integration with BambooHR
  • A connection to training completion and compliance tasks

Compliance Hub

Standards, including COMAR, are mapped to the requirements they create. Evidence and status sit with each requirement, alongside the staff credentials and tasks that support it. Audit views and reports bring that record together.

  • Standards mapped to specific requirements
  • Evidence and status for each requirement
  • Staff credentials and tasks tied to the standard they support
  • Audit views and reports for survey preparation

Staff Check Bot

Each month the bot screens active staff against the HHS-OIG exclusion list. It produces a tracking sheet and an evidence PDF with an attestation. Possible matches are flagged for HR to verify. A match is never confirmed automatically. SAM.gov and Maryland Medicaid checks are available.

  • Monthly HHS-OIG exclusion screening of active staff
  • A tracking sheet and an evidence PDF with attestation
  • Possible matches flagged for HR verification
  • A person makes the final call — the bot never auto-confirms a match
  • SAM.gov and Maryland Medicaid checks available

Audit Bot

The bot reviews charts and records against the documented rules of the program — psychiatric rehabilitation, Health Home, and outpatient. It flags gaps early and attaches the evidence behind each finding. Human review stays in charge of what happens next.

  • Audits against documented rules for PRP, Health Home, and outpatient programs
  • Gaps flagged early enough to correct
  • Findings backed by the evidence the bot used
  • A person reviews the results and decides the next step
