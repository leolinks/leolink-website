Staff Check Bot

Each month the bot screens active staff against the HHS-OIG exclusion list. It produces a tracking sheet and an evidence PDF with an attestation. Possible matches are flagged for HR to verify. A match is never confirmed automatically. SAM.gov and Maryland Medicaid checks are available.

Monthly exclusion screening, with a person confirming any possible match.

HR staff who run exclusion checks and keep the evidence.

HHS-OIG screening, with SAM.gov and Maryland Medicaid checks available.

  • Monthly HHS-OIG exclusion screening of active staff
  • A tracking sheet and an evidence PDF with attestation
  • Possible matches flagged for HR verification
  • A person makes the final call — the bot never auto-confirms a match
  • SAM.gov and Maryland Medicaid checks available

Audit Bot

The bot reviews charts and records against the documented rules of the program — psychiatric rehabilitation, Health Home, and outpatient. It flags gaps early and attaches the evidence behind each finding. Human review stays in charge of what happens next.

Chart and record review against program rules, with a person in charge of the result.

Quality and compliance reviewers who need gaps flagged before a survey.

Documented rules for PRP, Health Home, and outpatient programs.

  • Audits against documented rules for PRP, Health Home, and outpatient programs
  • Gaps flagged early enough to correct
  • Findings backed by the evidence the bot used
  • A person reviews the results and decides the next step
