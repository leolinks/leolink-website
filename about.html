About

Built around the work, by someone who has done the work.

LeoLink Services LLC exists to take the administrative load off teams, with custom web apps, careful automation, and support that fits how they already operate.

Founder & CEO

Lori B. Nedd

Lori B. Nedd founded LeoLink Services LLC in Owings Mills, Maryland. The firm grew from hands-on operational support — virtual assistance, business consulting, grant administration, and licensing and compliance — into a technology practice that builds the tools those organizations were missing.

The focus is business technology consulting and development. Behavioral and mental health practices are the specialty. Every other kind of business is welcome when the need is the same: less time on administration, and a record that can stand up to a question.
